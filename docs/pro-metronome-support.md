## What is Pro Metronome?

Pro Metronome is a highly customizable digital metronome for musicians of every level —
from beginners keeping a steady pulse to advanced players drilling complex subdivisions,
polyrhythms, and stage setlists. Beyond a basic click, it offers visual beat tracking,
accent control, vibration and flash cues, a Rhythm Trainer, a structured Practice Mode,
and a Playlist/Stage Mode for live performance.

The interface ships in three visual **themes** — **Legacy** (the default), **Vibrant**,
and **Dark** — which change the look but keep the same features and layout.

1. **Play / stop** — tap the big play button in the center of the screen.
   → *[Basics Pt.1 @ 0:22](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=22)*
2. **Set your tempo** — three ways (see below): the dial, type the BPM, or tap it out.
3. **Open Settings** — place a finger on the **left edge** of the screen and drag right.
   → *[Basics Pt.1 @ 0:56](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=56)*
4. **Shape the bar** — tap the accent bars at the top to set which beats are loud, soft, normal, or silent.

## 1. Playing and setting the tempo

### Start and stop
Tap the **large play button in the middle** of the screen to start the metronome; tap again to stop.
→ *[Basics Pt.1 @ 0:22](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=22)*

### Three ways to change the tempo
→ *[Basics Pt.1 @ 0:31](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=31)*

| Method | How |
|---|---|
| **Dial wheel** | Turn the tempo dial back and forth for fine, by-the-BPM adjustments. |
| **Type it in** | Tap the BPM number at the **top** of the screen to enter an exact tempo on the keypad. |
| **Tap Tempo** | Tap the **Tap Tempo** button at the bottom in time with the music; the app calculates the BPM from your taps. |

### Tempo calculation: BPM vs. quarter notes per minute
In **Settings → Tempo Calculation** you can switch the tempo unit between:
- **Beats per minute (BPM)** — the standard, used most of the time.
- **Quarter notes per minute (QPM)** — easier when working with **compound or unusual time signatures** (e.g. 3/8), because the felt pulse stays consistent. The *same* 3/8 setting feels faster in QPM and slower in BPM, so pick the unit that matches how you count.

→ *[Basics Pt.1 @ 4:58](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=298)* · *[Basics Pt.2 @ 1:03](https://youtu.be/3jnHEkCEd6k?t=63)*

## 2. The Settings panel

Open Settings by putting your finger on the **left side of the screen and dragging right**. Everything that customizes the metronome lives here, grouped into sections.
→ *[Basics Pt.1 @ 0:56](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=56)*

### Sound
→ *[Basics Pt.1 @ 1:04](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=64)*
- **Tone** — choose between several different click sounds.
- **Volume slider** — set the in-app volume independently of your device volume.
- **Button sound effect** — toggle on to hear a small click each time you change a setting (UI feedback); leave it off for silent operation.
- **AirPlay mode** — route the metronome audio over Apple AirPlay (iOS).

### Visual block
→ *[Basics Pt.1 @ 1:42](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=102)*
Controls what you see on screen while it plays:
- **Beat blocks** (on by default) — the bars that pulse with each beat. Uncheck to show nothing; most players keep it on so they can *see* the beat.
- **Pendulum** — a swinging pendulum visualization instead of/with the blocks.
- **Highlight beats (lights)** — flash colored lights on certain accent types:
  - **Accent → red light** on the main accent. → *[3:05](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=185)*
  - **Sub accent → yellow light**.
  - **Normal → green light**.
- **Bar counter** — shows a running count of how many bars have played.
  → *[3:45](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=225)*

### Vibration (haptics)
→ *[Basics Pt.1 @ 4:05](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=245)*
- **Haptic vibration** — the phone vibrates slightly on every pulse.
- **Vibration on accent** — only the accented beats vibrate.

### Flash
→ *[Basics Pt.1 @ 4:20](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=260)*
Same idea as vibration, but with light instead:
- **Flash screen** — the screen flashes on the beat.
- **Flash LED** — the phone's **front-facing camera light** flashes on every first beat. Availability depends on your device having a usable camera light.

### Playback
→ *[Basics Pt.1 @ 4:42](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=282)*
- **Count-in bars** — choose how many bars to count in before the metronome "really" begins. Handy for timing practice sessions and giving yourself a lead-in.

### Tempo calculation
The unit switch (BPM ↔ QPM) described in §1.

## 3. Time signatures, accents, subdivisions & polyrhythms

These are the "advanced" tools intermediate and advanced players use most.
→ *[Basics Pt.2 — full video](https://youtu.be/3jnHEkCEd6k)*

### Time signature
At the top of the screen you'll see the time signature (e.g. **4/4**), labeled **TS**. Tap it to edit.
→ *[Basics Pt.2 @ 0:40](https://youtu.be/3jnHEkCEd6k?t=40)*
- **Beats (top number / numerator)** — use **+ / −** to raise or lower how many beats per bar.
- **Note value (bottom number / denominator)** — set the beat unit; it goes up to **32**.
- For odd meters like **3/8**, switch **Tempo Calculation to quarter notes per minute** so the pulse feels right.

### Accents
Below the time signature you'll find the **accents** row. Tap a beat to cycle its intensity:
**full accent → sub accent → normal → silent (note removed)**.
You can also set accents straight from the home screen by tapping the beat blocks at the top.
→ *[Basics Pt.2 @ 1:41](https://youtu.be/3jnHEkCEd6k?t=101)* · *[Basics Pt.1 @ 2:18](https://youtu.be/2ZIkl4mPSkQ?t=138)*

> **Accent levels at a glance:** full (loudest, all blocks filled), sub accent (medium), normal (single block), and muted/silent (no sound, useful for rests).

### Subdivisions
Tap the **time signature (4/4)** at the top to open the panel, then choose a subdivision. There are **two pages** of options, including:
- Quarter note (default)
- Eighth note, off-beat eighth
- Triplets, off-beat triplets
- Dotted-note subdivisions, and more.

Tapping **"Time signature"** or **"Subdivision"** both open the same panel.
→ *[Basics Pt.2 @ 2:11](https://youtu.be/3jnHEkCEd6k?t=131)*

### Polyrhythms
On the time-signature / subdivision panel, flip the **Polyrhythm** switch at the bottom. This **replaces the fixed subdivision menu** with full manual control of the two rhythmic layers (that's why both live on the same panel), plus an on-screen visualization to help you lock in. Examples shown: **4:5**, **4:7**, and the classic **3:2**.
→ *[Basics Pt.2 @ 3:01](https://youtu.be/3jnHEkCEd6k?t=181)*

## 4. Rhythm Trainer (ghost clicks)

The Rhythm Trainer **mutes certain beats** ("ghost clicks") so you have to keep time on your own until the click returns. Over time this builds an internal sense of pulse that's independent of the metronome.
→ *[Rhythm Trainer — full video](https://youtu.be/cQt_bPkkGhg)* · *concept @ [0:30](https://youtu.be/cQt_bPkkGhg?t=30)*

### Turn it on
1. Open the **Settings** panel (button on the left).
2. Scroll down to **Rhythm Trainer** and toggle it on.
3. A **shortcut** then appears at the top, above the time-signature (e.g. 4/4) marking — tap it to open the trainer anytime.
   - In the **Vibrant theme**, find it via the **tools** icon on the left instead.

→ *[Rhythm Trainer @ 0:48](https://youtu.be/cQt_bPkkGhg?t=48)*

### Muting modes
- **Fixed** — mutes at the **same place every time** the bar repeats (predictable; good for first attempts).
  → *[1:46](https://youtu.be/cQt_bPkkGhg?t=106)*
- **Randomized** — mutes in **random places**, so you must stay locked in through unpredictable gaps.
  → *[2:41](https://youtu.be/cQt_bPkkGhg?t=161)*

### Show visuals over muted bars
By default the on-screen visualization keeps showing the click even while beats are muted. Toggle **"Show visuals over muted bars"** off to hide it — forcing you to rely entirely on your own timing.
→ *[3:21](https://youtu.be/cQt_bPkkGhg?t=201)*

## 5. Practice Mode (gradual tempo training)

Practice Mode starts you slow and **gradually works the tempo up** to a goal, so you can groove a sticking, lick, or pattern in cleanly without stopping to bump the tempo by hand.
→ *[Practice Mode — full video](https://youtu.be/DFF5U-99spk)*

### Open it and create a sequence
- Tap the **Practice Mode** button at the **bottom-middle** of the screen. (If you have no saved sequences yet, the list is empty.)
- Tap **+** at the top to create a new sequence and give it a name.
→ *[Practice Mode @ 0:46](https://youtu.be/DFF5U-99spk?t=46)*

There are **two modes** that reach a goal tempo in different ways:

### A) Warm-up mode
You define **sections**, each a percentage of the goal tempo held for a set duration.
→ *[1:14](https://youtu.be/DFF5U-99spk?t=74)*
1. Set the **goal tempo** (e.g. 110 BPM — tap the number for a keypad).
2. Set **count-in bars** (default 1).
3. **Section 1:** choose a starting percentage (e.g. **50%** of 110 = 55 BPM) and a **duration in bars** (the app shows the time equivalent, e.g. 14 bars ≈ 1 minute). After that section it steps up to the next.
4. **Add a section** to add more steps (e.g. 70%, then 80%), each with its own duration.
5. **Rhythmic settings** (optional): turn on **Customize time signature** to change the meter (e.g. 3/4), the **subdivision** (eighth, off-beat, triplet…), or a **polyrhythm** for this sequence.
6. Press **Start** and play along — the tempo climbs through your sections automatically.

### B) Automator mode
Instead of percentage sections, the app **bumps the tempo by a fixed amount at a fixed interval**.
→ *[4:08](https://youtu.be/DFF5U-99spk?t=248)*
1. Set the **target tempo** (e.g. 130 BPM) and the **start tempo** (e.g. 90 BPM). Tap any tempo number for a keypad.
2. Choose the **interval unit** — bars, minutes, or seconds — and how often the tempo bumps (e.g. every 4 bars).
3. Choose the **increase amount** — e.g. **+5 beats** each interval (up to +10).
4. Set **count-in bars** and optional **rhythmic settings** as above.
5. Start — the metronome accelerates step by step from start to target.

### Manage saved sequences
Saved sessions are listed when you reopen Practice Mode. Tap the **three-dots (⋯)** menu on a session to **Edit**, **Duplicate**, or **Delete** it.
→ *[6:07](https://youtu.be/DFF5U-99spk?t=367)*

## 6. Playlist Mode & Stage Mode (for rehearsal and live shows)

Build a **playlist of songs**, each with its own tempo and beat settings, then jump from one to the next with a single tap — ideal for setlists.
→ *[Playlist & Stage — full video](https://youtu.be/9IWHAxfCYcA)*

> On first open you'll see a **sample playlist** the app provides as a demo, to show how it works — you can use it, edit it, or delete it (Edit → select → red delete) and build your own.

### Create a playlist
1. Tap the **Playlist** button at the bottom.
2. Tap the **list-management button on the right** → **Playlist** → **New List** (bottom-left), and name it.
→ *[1:02](https://youtu.be/9IWHAxfCYcA?t=62)*

### Add songs
- Inside a playlist, tap **New Song**, give it a name and **BPM**.
- Scroll down to set how the beats are played — tap the per-beat buttons to choose **accent / softer accent / no accent / rest**. A **rest** silences that beat, so you can build count patterns — e.g. a song that clicks only on beats **2-3-4** (beat 1 silent), with an accent added on beat 3.
- **Save.** Repeat for each song.
→ *[1:23](https://youtu.be/9IWHAxfCYcA?t=83)* · *per-beat accents @ [2:00](https://youtu.be/9IWHAxfCYcA?t=120)*

Tap any song to load its tempo and settings instantly.

### Switch settings (how songs change)
Tap **Switch Settings** (bottom-left):
→ *[3:06](https://youtu.be/9IWHAxfCYcA?t=186)*
- **Immediately** — the next song starts the moment you tap it (cuts mid-bar).
- **On the next bar** — tap the next song any time mid-bar and it waits, finishing the current bar before switching (avoids a jarring jump on stage).
- **Auto switch** — each song advances on its own after a set number of bars (**default ×4**). Because real songs are far longer, set each song's **Repeated bars** (below) so it advances at the true song length instead of every 4 bars.
- **Loop list** — restarts the playlist from the top when it reaches the end.

### Repeated bars per song
Since real songs aren't 4 bars long, open **Edit**, tap a song, and set its **Repeated bars** (default 4; a typical song is ~140-160). After that many bars it advances to the next song.
→ *[4:33](https://youtu.be/9IWHAxfCYcA?t=273)*

### Quick override
Changed a song's BPM on the fly and want to keep it? **Press and hold** the song to get the **Override** option and save the new setting.
→ *[5:02](https://youtu.be/9IWHAxfCYcA?t=302)*

### Manage multiple playlists
Go to **Edit → Playlist** (top) to see all your lists. Tap **Edit** at the bottom to **reorder**, **delete** (red button), or **duplicate** a playlist.
→ *[5:15](https://youtu.be/9IWHAxfCYcA?t=315)*

### Stage Mode
Tap **Stage Mode** at the bottom for a stripped-down, gig-friendly view:
→ *[6:09](https://youtu.be/9IWHAxfCYcA?t=369)*
- Songs become **big buttons** that are easy to hit on stage.
- **Playback control** at the bottom: turn **Auto switch** on/off (on = advance after the set bars; off = click until you tap the next song), and use **Auto stop** to stop after a chosen number of bars (e.g. 4).

### Share / import playlists
- To share: open the **Edit** tool → **Share list** → send it anywhere — to your **laptop**, **message it to a bandmate**, or **Save to Files**.
- To import: open the shared file and it launches Pro Metronome. Choose an **existing list** to add the songs to, or **Create an import** to make a new list.
→ *[7:03](https://youtu.be/9IWHAxfCYcA?t=423)*

## 7. Timer

Practice for a set length of time or enforce time limits.
→ *[Basics Pt.2 @ 4:07](https://youtu.be/3jnHEkCEd6k?t=247)*
- Tap the **timer** button at the top and set the **duration**.
- Three options:
  - **Start metronome simultaneously** — the click starts when the timer starts.
  - **Stop metronome when finished** — the click stops when the timer ends.
  - **Repeat timer** — the timer restarts automatically when it runs out.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Pro Metronome free? What platforms?**
Yes — it's free on both **iOS (App Store)** and **Android (Google Play)**.

**How do I change the tempo precisely?**
Three ways: turn the **dial**, **tap the BPM number** to type an exact value, or use **Tap Tempo** to tap it out. (§1)

**How do I open the settings?**
Place a finger on the **left edge** of the screen and drag right. (§2)

**Why does my odd time signature (like 3/8) feel too slow or too fast?**
Switch **Tempo Calculation** between **BPM** and **quarter notes per minute** in Settings. QPM usually feels right for compound/odd meters. (§1, §3)

**How do I accent specific beats?**
Tap the **beat blocks at the top** of the home screen, or open the **Accents** row in the time-signature panel, and cycle each beat through full → sub accent → normal → silent. (§3)

**How do I silence a beat / add a rest?**
Cycle that beat down to its **silent/muted** level (in Accents, or by tapping the beat block). In Playlist songs you can also set a beat to **rest**. (§3, §6)

**Can it do triplets, off-beats, and dotted rhythms?**
Yes — open **Subdivisions** (two pages of options): eighths, off-beat eighths, triplets, off-beat triplets, dotted notes, and more. (§3)

**Can it do polyrhythms like 3:2 or 4:5?**
Yes — flip the **Polyrhythm** switch on the subdivision panel and set both layers; an on-screen visualization helps. (§3)

**What is the Rhythm Trainer / ghost clicks?**
It mutes some beats so you keep time yourself until the click returns — training your internal pulse. It has **fixed** and **randomized** muting, plus a "show visuals over muted bars" toggle. (§4)

**How do I gradually speed up while practicing?**
Use **Practice Mode**. **Warm-up** mode steps through percentage-of-goal sections; **Automator** mode bumps the tempo by a fixed amount every N bars/minutes. (§5)

**Can I make a setlist with different tempos per song?**
Yes — **Playlist Mode**. Add songs with their own BPM and beat settings, set repeated bars, and switch immediately, on the next bar, or automatically. (§6)

**I'm playing live — is there a simpler view?**
Yes — **Stage Mode** turns songs into big buttons with auto-switch and auto-stop controls. (§6)

**Can I share my playlists with bandmates?**
Yes — **Share list** exports the file (send to a laptop, message, or Save to Files); opening it on another device imports into Pro Metronome. (§6)

**Does it vibrate or flash instead of (or with) sound?**
Yes — enable **Haptic vibration** / **Vibration on accent**, and/or **Flash screen** / **Flash LED** (front-facing camera light on beat 1). (§2)

**Can I use a foot pedal?**
Yes — pair a compatible external pedal under **Profile → Advanced settings → Foot pedal**. (§9)

**How do I count in before starting?**
Set **Count-in bars** in **Settings → Playback** (and Practice Mode sequences have their own count-in). (§2, §5)

**Can I time my practice sessions?**
Yes — use the **Timer** (duration + start/stop/repeat options), or count-in bars to mark session starts. (§7)

**The visualization is hard to see from a distance.**
Switch to **Landscape mode** for much larger beat blocks. (§8)

**How do I change how the app looks?**
Pick **Legacy**, **Vibrant**, or **Dark** in **Profile settings**. (§10)

**Where can I get more help?**
Email the EUMLab team at **team@eumlab.com**.
